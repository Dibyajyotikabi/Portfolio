Why I made these

Kids today pick up a phone before they pick up a pencil. You've probably seen it at home, or at a relative's place.

Most worksheet packs online are made for kids in the US or UK. Snowmen, dollars, pumpkins. That's not what an Indian kid counts every day.

So I made sheets with things your child already knows. Mangoes, autos, rupee coins, and Hindi right next to the ABCs.

Each sheet is short. One a day is enough to build the habit.

How to use them

  1. Print on A4 with "Fit to page" or "Actual size". Black and white works fine. No printer? Send the PDF to a print shop on WhatsApp or a pen drive. Most charge ₹2 to ₹5 a page.
  2. Keep it short. Age 3 to 4 needs one sheet for 5 to 10 minutes. Age 5 to 6, one or two sheets for 10 to 15 minutes. Age 7 to 8, two sheets for 15 to 20 minutes.
  3. Give a thick pencil or a jumbo crayon. Wobbly lines are normal at this age, so don't erase them.
  4. Hand out stars. Every sheet has three: one for trying, one for finishing, one for neat work. The answer key is at the end of each pack.

The sheets in each pack go from easy to harder, so just start at page one.
