Why I wrote this

For a few years, I wrote Android guides from home. Rooting tutorials, firmware updates, all of it. Later, one of those sites became a real business.

The skill that paid wasn't really Android. It was writing something useful, putting it online, and letting people find it through Google.

This book is that whole process, written down step by step. No coding, no big budget. Just a laptop, an internet connection and some patience.

Look inside

Real pages from the book. Every setup step has a screenshot, and every price has a source link.

What's inside

  1. The truth about blogging in 2026. What changed with AI answers in Google, what still works, and the seven ways blogs actually make money.
  2. Building your blog. Picking a niche that can pay, choosing a domain and hosting, setting up WordPress, and the pages every money blog needs.
  3. Writing content that ranks. Keyword research with free tools, a simple post template, using AI without hurting your blog, and SEO in plain English.
  4. Getting traffic. Your first 1,000 visitors from Google, Bing, Pinterest and forums, plus an email list from day one.
  5. Making money. AdSense to Mediavine and Raptive, affiliate marketing done honestly, your own digital products, and sponsored work.
  6. Growing into a real income. A 12-month roadmap, the 10 mistakes that cost bloggers months, and taxes, payments and backups.

At the end there's a toolkit: a niche scoring sheet, a 30-day launch plan, a before-you-publish checklist, an AdSense checklist, the tools I recommend, and a glossary.

Who it's for

  • You want to earn from home and you've never built a website.
  • You started a blog once, got no traffic, and gave up.
  • You write well and want that skill to pay you something.

It's not for you if you want fast money. Most blogs take 6 to 12 months before they earn anything meaningful. I say that in the book too.
