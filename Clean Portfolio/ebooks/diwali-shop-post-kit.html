Why I made this

Every festival, most shops post the same forwarded greeting. Your customers have seen it ten times already, and it doesn't even have your shop's name on it.

A designer can make you a few custom posts, but that usually costs ₹500 to ₹1,000. And by the time they're ready, Dhanteras is gone.

So I made a set that's ready today. You add your name and number, and every post looks like your shop made it.

Here's why it matters. When someone needs a gift, a sweet box or a new phone, they go to the shop they remember.

How it works

  1. Download the zip from your email and unzip it. It's about 12MB.
  2. Open the Post Maker in Chrome. Type your shop name, phone number and area. It needs internet the first time, to load the fonts.
  3. Pick a design and save it. Square for Instagram and Facebook, tall for WhatsApp Status.
  4. Copy a caption from the captions file and post. Check the calendar in the guide to see which post goes out on which day.

Only have a phone? The guide shows you how to add your shop name with your phone's own Gallery editor. No app to install.

Questions

Do I need Photoshop or Canva?

No. The Post Maker runs in Chrome. You can also use the ready images with your phone's Gallery editor, and the guide shows you how.

Does the Post Maker work on a phone?

It works best in Chrome on a laptop or computer. On a phone only, use the Gallery method from the guide. It takes about 2 minutes per post.

Which festivals does it cover?

Dhanteras, Choti Diwali, Diwali, Lakshmi Puja, Govardhan Puja, Bhai Dooj and Chhath, plus wedding season, new stock, open late and thank you posts you can use after the festival.

Are the captions in Hindi?

Yes. There are 104 captions in Hindi and English, so you can post in whatever your customers read.

Can I share the kit with other shop owners?

The kit is for your own shop. Please don't resell the designs or share the zip in WhatsApp groups.

How do I get the files?

The zip arrives in your email right after payment. If you don't see the email in a few minutes, check your spam folder.
