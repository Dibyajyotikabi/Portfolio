Why I made this

Teachers I know spend their evenings on lesson plans, worksheets and report card remarks. The teaching happens in class. The paperwork follows them home.

ChatGPT and Gemini can do a lot of that paperwork. But most prompt guides online are in English and made for schools abroad.

So I wrote 110 prompts in simple Hindi, for Indian classrooms from Class 1 to 10. You fill in the class, subject and topic, and send it.

That's it. No new app, no subscription, no tech skills.

How to use it

  1. Open the copy file on your phone, or the PDF on your computer.
  2. Pick a prompt and copy it. Fill in the blanks: class, subject, topic and how long the period is.
  3. Paste it into free ChatGPT or Gemini and send.
  4. Read the answer, fix anything that's off, and use it. Ask a follow-up like "make it easier" if you need to.

New to AI? Start with the cheat sheet and the 7 day plan in chapter 12. One prompt a day is enough.

Questions

Do I need to pay for ChatGPT or Gemini?

No. Every prompt works on the free versions of ChatGPT and Gemini.

Is the whole kit in Hindi?

Yes. The PDF and the prompts are in simple Hindi. The answers you get back will be in Hindi too.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The kit comes with a plain text file, so you can copy any prompt on your phone and paste it into the ChatGPT or Gemini app.

Which classes and subjects does it cover?

Class 1 to 10. Chapter 8 has prompts by subject, and most of the other prompts work for any subject once you fill in the topic.

Is it safe to use student details?

Don't paste full names, marks or phone numbers into AI tools. Chapter 11 shows you how to write report card remarks without them.

The kit is for your own use. Please don't share the files or resell them. If your colleagues like it, send them this page.

The PDF and the copy file arrive in your email right after payment. If you don't see the email in a few minutes, check your spam folder.
