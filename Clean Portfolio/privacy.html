Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 26, 2026

The short version

This site collects as little as possible. There are no ad trackers here, and I don't sell or share your personal data.

When you visit

  • Your theme choice (Light, Dark or Read) is saved in a cookie and your browser's local storage.
  • The visitor counter uses an anonymous, signed cookie so a refresh isn't counted twice. It doesn't store your name, IP address or anything that identifies you.
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and served through Cloudflare. Like any web host, they may keep standard server logs, such as IP address and browser type, for security.

When you buy an ebook

Checkout is handled by my payment partner. They collect your name, email, billing country and payment details to process the order, send the download and handle tax. Your payment details go to them, not to me. I receive your name, email and order details so I can support you.

When you contact me or subscribe

If you email me or use the subscribe form (which opens your own email app), I get your email address and whatever you write. I use it only to reply to you or send the updates you asked for. You can ask me to stop at any time.

Links to other sites

Pages here link to other sites, like my blog, GitHub, LinkedIn, Cal.com and tools I recommend. Those sites have their own privacy policies.

Your rights

You can ask me what data I have about you, ask me to correct it, or ask me to delete it. Email dibyajyotikabi@gmail.com and I'll reply within 30 days.
