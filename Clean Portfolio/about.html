About me

I’m Dibyajyoti Kabi, a developer, automation builder, and publishing operator based in Bhubaneswar, Odisha, India.

ExperienceEducationCertificationsServicesTools

I’ve spent 10+ years building for the web: WordPress sites, custom plugins, publishing platforms, and the systems that keep them running.

I’ve shipped 120+ projects, including themes, plugins, audits, and fixes, with 70% faster loading on average across the sites I’ve optimised. My experience includes 3+ years working with agent workflows.

I founded RootMyGalaxy, grew it past 10M+ pageviews, and sold it for $150,000. That experience shaped how I work. A useful website needs good content, a reliable technical foundation, and a publishing process people can actually keep up with.

Today, I’m the director at RMG Media Group and run operations at TheDroidGuru. Alongside that, I build my own projects and help teams with WordPress, technical SEO, and automation.

Lately, I’ve been spending more time on practical agent systems: Hermes workflows, OpenClaw gateways, Grok Bot, MCP tools, and setups around Claude, Codex, and Gemini. My goal is straightforward: less busywork, clearer steps, and automation the team can trust.
