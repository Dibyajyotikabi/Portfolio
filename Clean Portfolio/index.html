Hi, I’m Dibyajyoti Kabi.

I’m a developer, automation builder, and publishing operator with a soft spot for making the repetitive stuff run itself. I turn “there has to be an easier way” into websites, bots, and workflows that give people their time back.

Lately, that means working with Hermes, OpenClaw, and Grok Bot, and finding new ways to put AI & automation to work. My roots are in WordPress and publishing: I built and sold RootMyGalaxy for $150,000, and now manage RMG Media Group and operations at TheDroidGuru.

I also build things I’d want to use myself: SamUpdater for Samsung updates, AI Update Notes for keeping up with AI, and a live Cricket Scoreboard for following the game.
